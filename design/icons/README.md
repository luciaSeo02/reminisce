# App icon source

`icon-source.svg` is the full bleed source of the app icon (heart on terracotta). It is kept here only for future exports and is not served or referenced by the app.

The exported files the app uses live in `content/` (served at the site root): `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 px), `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px), `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `icon-maskable-512.png`.

Colors: background `#B4532A`, heart `#FFFCF6`, inner heart `#F8DCC8`.

Notes for future exports:
- `apple-touch-icon.png` is full bleed on purpose. iOS rounds the corners itself.
- `icon-maskable-512.png` is full bleed, with the heart inside the maskable safe zone (the central circle, 80% of the size).
- Do not redesign or recolor the icons without the owner's approval.
